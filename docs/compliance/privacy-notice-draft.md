## 1. Introduction

Pav Dental is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal and clinical health data is handled securely and transparently in full accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018), and General Dental Council (GDC) ethical standards.

This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information when you use our mobile app, visit our clinics, book a mobile dental van, or participate in a virtual dental consultation.

---

## 2. Information We Collect About You

### 2.1 Information You Provide to Us
- **Identity & Contact Details:** Full name, date of birth, biological sex or gender identity, home address, billing address, email address, and mobile phone number.
- **Emergency Contact & Next of Kin:** Name, relationship, and contact telephone number.
- **Dependants & Family:** Details of children or legal dependants linked to your account for pediatric dental appointments.
- **Medical & Dental History:** Existing medical conditions, current medications, drug and latex allergies, dental phobias, smoking/alcohol habits, and details of your registered General Practitioner (GP).
- **Clinical Records:** Dental examination notes, digital tooth charts (odontograms), treatment estimates, accepted treatment plans, radiographs (X-rays), intraoral photographs, and electronic prescriptions.
- **Communications:** Secure in-app messages exchanged with our practice team and clinicians.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device & App Diagnostics:** Operating system version, anonymised device identifiers, app performance diagnostics, and crash logs (with all personal data scrubbed).
- **Location Information:** Your postcode when searching for mobile van coverage; van dispatch utilizes approximate location to coordinate arrival times.

## 4. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with trusted third parties strictly necessary to deliver dental care:
1. **Registered Dental Clinicians:** Clinicians assigned to your care have time-limited access to your dental chart and medical history during the appointment window.
2. **Dispensing Pharmacies:** Electronic prescription bundles (digitally signed by your prescribing dentist) are transmitted to accredited UK pharmacies for dispensing.
3. **General Practitioners & Hospitals:** Where urgent referral or medical clearance is clinically required, records are shared with your consent (or under vital interests in life-threatening emergencies).
4. **Third-Party Service Providers (Sub-Processors):**
   - **Stripe:** PCI-DSS Level 1 payment processing.
   - **Amazon Web Services (AWS):** ISO 27001-certified UK cloud hosting (London region) with KMS encryption at rest.
   - **Daily.co:** Encrypted WebRTC virtual consultation video streams.
   - **Twilio & Postmark:** Delivering two-factor authentication SMS codes and transactional appointment emails.

## 5. How Long We Keep Your Data

In compliance with GDC Standards and NHS Records Management Codes of Practice:
- **Adult Patients:** Complete clinical records, odontograms, radiographs, and notes are retained for a minimum of **11 years** from the date of your last treatment.
- **Children and Young People:** Retained for **11 years** from the last treatment date, or until the patient reaches **25 years of age** (whichever is longer).
- **Financial & Payment Records:** Retained for 7 years in compliance with HMRC statutory accounting rules.

When you request account deletion, your login credentials and personal marketing preferences are erased immediately; clinical records remain securely archived in an encrypted, read-only vault until the statutory retention period expires.

## 6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the following statutory rights regarding your personal data:
- **Right of Access (Subject Access Request):** You can download a complete authenticated copy of your treatment plans, odontogram history, documents, and signed consents directly via the Document Vault in the app, or request a complete export by emailing `dpo@pavdental.com`.
- **Right to Rectification:** You can update your contact details, emergency contacts, and dependants directly in the app settings, or request corrections to factual errors.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You may request account deletion at any time via App Settings > Profile > Delete Account. Clinical records required by law are retained under statutory clinical retention rules.
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** You can ask us to pause processing of your personal data under certain circumstances.
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** If you believe we have not handled your personal data in accordance with the law, you have the right to complain to the UK supervisory authority:  
  **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**  
  Website: `https://ico.org.uk`  
  Telephone: 0303 123 1113
